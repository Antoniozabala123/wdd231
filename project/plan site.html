Home Designs About Us

1. Site Name

Name: Shopping Shoes

Project Subject: An online e-commerce platform and wholesale distribution network specializing in footwear, sports sneakers, and urban shoes in Venezuela.

Reason for Selection: This name is direct, highly recognizable, and optimized for search visibility regarding digital shoe stores and wholesale footwear indexing within the national market.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this project is to centralize access to trendy, high-quality footwear and streamline the wholesale sourcing process for local clothing entrepreneurs in Venezuela, who often struggle to find reliable distributors. The site operates as a modern digital shoe store featuring responsive grid layouts for product catalogs and collections.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

To optimize user experience, the website content answers the following target audience questions:

  • Scenario 1: "As a wholesale clothing entrepreneur in Maracaibo, how can I find a verified footwear distributor, read their business description, and access their catalog easily on my phone?"
  • Scenario 2: "As an urban shoe shopper in Caracas, how can I locate the physical address or operational details of a store and access the 'Learn More' action link cleanly on a desktop viewport?"

4. Color Schema

The site utilizes the following colors to establish corporate identity and maintain proper accessibility levels:

  • Primary Color (#0d47a1 - Deep Blue): Used for structural headers, primary section banners, and major typography highlights.
  • Secondary Color (#2196f3 - Light Blue): Applied to navigation elements, active focus links, and card borders.
  • Background Neutral (#eee - Light Gray): Applied directly to individual business card containers to isolate content cleanly.

5. Typography

The typography choices ensure clean text rendering across all device breakpoints:

  • Primary Font: 'Roboto', sans-serif
  • Headings: Roboto Bold (700) for h1 and h2
  • Body Text: Roboto Regular (400) for paragraphs, addresses, and descriptions

6. Wireframes

Structural layouts planned for the responsive system:

Small Screen (320px 640px)

Single vertical column layout. Cards stack linearly: Name → Image → Address → Description → Button. No horizontal overflow.

Large Screen (1025px and above)

Three-column layout using named grid areas (Photo | Information | Side button) to maximize horizontal space smoothly.
